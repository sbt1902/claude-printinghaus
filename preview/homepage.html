03 / Print methods & finishes

Printing methods, done properly.

From fast, full-colour digital to traditional letterpress, we match the method to the piece.

01

Digital

Sharp, vibrant and quick. Ideal for full-colour designs and shorter runs.

02

Offset

Precise, consistent colour for larger runs and brand-critical work.

03

Letterpress

Ink pressed deep into soft cotton paper for an impression you can feel.

04

Foil stamping

Metallic and pigment foils that catch the light: gold, copper, silver and more.

05

Embossing

Raised or recessed detail, with or without ink, for quiet, tactile impact.

06

Painted edges

A flash of colour along the edge of thick stocks. A small detail with a big effect.
